Parkinson's Disease Prevention & Neural Stabilization Device

NEURO DAWN is an innovative biomedical system developed to detect and mitigate Parkinson’s disease early stages through non-invasive neuro-telemetry and micro-current brain stimulation.

Brainwave & Dopamine Telemetry:

A specialized recording module designed to capture and analyze brain activity patterns and dopamine level indicators across meditation, attention/focus, and stress states.

Controlled Electrical Neuro-Stimulation:

Applies calibrated micro-electrical currents to stimulate targeted brain regions (specifically the substantia nigra) to boost and stabilize dopamine production.
